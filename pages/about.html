Why West Farm?

——

The name Westfarm Resort is inspired by the resort’s location in Barangay Dumpay West, Basista, Pangasinan.

The word “West” represents our connection to the community where the resort is rooted, while “Farm” reflects our vision of creating a nature-inspired destination that embraces the beauty, tranquility, and character of the countryside.
