Need to Know

Frequently
Asked

Have more questions? Reach us at westfarmresort@gmail.com

We are located in Barangay Dumpay West, Basista, Pangasinan — just 4km and 7 minutes from the Basista Public Market. Easily accessible by private vehicle.
Small pets weighing 10 kilos and below are allowed on the property, subject to certain terms and conditions. Please keep them leashed and be responsible for their care.
Outside food and delivery are not prohibited. Cooking is also allowed inside the cabin or villa. We also encourage you to try our in-house West Crays restaurant!
We accept Cash and GCash payments. Bank transfers are not yet available. For reservations, a down payment may be required to confirm your booking.
Check-in time is flexible depending on your arrival. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability. Please coordinate with our staff in advance.
