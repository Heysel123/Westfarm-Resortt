About Westfarm Resort
Westfarm Resort is a nature-inspired destination designed to give guests a relaxing and memorable countryside experience. We aim to bring together nature, comfort, recreation, and warm hospitality in one welcoming place.
The name Westfarm is inspired by our location in Barangay Dumpay West, Basista, Pangasinan. "West" represents our roots and connection to our local community, while "Farm" reflects our vision of creating a nature-inspired resort that celebrates the beauty and tranquility of the countryside.
Westfarm Resort is located in Roxas Street, Barangay Dumpay West, Basista, Pangasinan. You can find us on Google Maps here.
Westfarm Resort is designed to be more than just a place to stay. It is a place where guests can slow down, enjoy nature, spend quality time with loved ones, and create lasting memories.
